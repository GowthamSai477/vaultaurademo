Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft · will be finalized before public release

The short version: VaultAura is built so your data never reaches us. Passwords, vault contents, and keys stay on your devices. We collect almost nothing — and while the product is in development, this page describes exactly what that means.

What we collect today

VaultAura has not launched. The only personal data this website collects is an email address you voluntarily submit to the launch-notification list. It is used solely to send you one notification when VaultAura launches (and nothing else, unless you later opt in).

What the product will never collect

  • Your passwords, vault entries, or credential metadata
  • Encryption keys — these are derived on your devices and never leave them
  • Browsing history, form contents, or screen contents beyond the autofill action you trigger

Zero-knowledge sync

When sync launches, it will be end-to-end encrypted and zero-knowledge: vault data is encrypted on your device with keys we do not hold, so synced blobs are unreadable to us.

Analytics & cookies

This pre-launch site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. If privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics are added later, this page will be updated first.

Contact

Questions or data requests: hello@vaultaura.in. Security issues: security@vaultaura.in.
